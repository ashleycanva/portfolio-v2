The challenge

50 stores, no plan

MögeTee was opening US franchises faster than its marketing could follow. Each store ran on its own, with no shared messaging, weak social accounts, no influencer or paid ad strategy, and no way to get national promotions to every location.

01

An influencer network from scratch

Recruited 30+ micro-influencers (food lovers, lifestyle creators and local figures) on commission and free product instead of expensive paid deals.

30+ partners · 3,000+ followers in 4 months · low cost

02

"Be the Mochi to my Berry"

Created a Valentine's campaign around a new Mochi and Berry drink pairing, then packaged it as a ready-to-use kit with messaging and recipes for every participating US store.

Original concept · franchise-wide rollout

03

The first paid ad program

Proposed and launched the brand's first structured Facebook and Instagram ads, A/B testing image, video and carousel formats to guide future creative.

A/B tested formats · data-driven creative

04

Merch for store activations

Developed keychains, figures, balloons and flowers for openings and raffles, working directly with manufacturers in China from prototype to delivery.

Vendor-managed from prototype to delivery

MögeTee went from a scattered, low-engagement presence to 3,000+ new followers, 30+ influencer partners and its first nationally coordinated seasonal campaign, with the systems in place to keep growing.
