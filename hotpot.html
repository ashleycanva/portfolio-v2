The challenge

Selling something new

Most Houston diners had never tried individual hotpot, so the restaurant had to explain the concept before it could sell it. It was competing with well-loved Chinatown names, with almost no online presence and no marketing in place.

01

Paid ads & social growth

Took over Instagram, built the Facebook page from scratch, and ran Houston ads aimed at food lovers and Chinatown regulars, with retargeting for past visitors.

$400/month · 1,000+ followers from ads alone

02

Affiliates & community

Partnered with local food bloggers and influencers through tastings and referral incentives, and landed features in Houston's Got Spice and Houston Eatery.

Influencer network · word of mouth · local press

03

Content & collateral

Shot food photos and video in-house and designed posters for new dishes and seasonal drinks, so first-timers understood the concept right away.

Food photography · promo posters · one visual identity

In one of Houston's most competitive dining neighborhoods, the restaurant went from 200 followers to 3,000+, with an active affiliate network and a steady stream of content, all on a tight budget.
