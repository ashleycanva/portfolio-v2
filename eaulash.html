The challenge

A crowded market

The lash market was dominated by DoeBaby, HouseOfLash and Glamnetic, with loyal audiences and big ad budgets. I had no brand, no audience and no product, so everything had to be built at once, on a lean budget.

01

Brand & product

Sourced an adhesive eyeliner that doubles as lash glue, a 1-step product few competitors offered, then designed the logo, colors, packaging and social templates.

2-in-1 product · full brand kit · e-commerce store

02

Content creation

Made reels, tutorials and product demos for women 18–35 on Instagram and TikTok, using Canva and editing tools to keep quality high on a small budget.

Reels · tutorials · one visual identity

03

Ads & social growth

Ran Facebook and Instagram ads with A/B tests on creative and audiences, and grew organic followers to 1,300+ in the first few months after launch.

A/B tested ads · 1,300+ organic followers

04

Influencers & affiliates

Seeded product with beauty micro-influencers and set up an affiliate program, coordinating content schedules to drive traffic to the store.

Product seeding · affiliate referrals

A complete brand from concept to market: product, identity, store and an audience of 1,300+, with paid ads, organic content and affiliates all running in parallel.
